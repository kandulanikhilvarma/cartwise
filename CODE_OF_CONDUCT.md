# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Critique the code, the design or the claim — not the person who wrote it.
- Say what is wrong and what would be right. "This is broken" without a
  reason is noise; "this reports the wrong mass for multi-pack lines, here
  is a receipt that shows it" is a contribution.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. Cartwise refuses features that would
  make it guess at numbers, and that refusal is the project working.
- Respect that people contribute in different languages, timezones and
  amounts of free time.

## What is not

- Harassment, slurs, or sustained hostility in issues, pull requests or
  commit messages.
- Sexual attention or imagery anywhere in the project.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately filing noise: spam issues, drive-by AI-generated pull requests
  that nobody has read, or reopening a settled decision to wear people down.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and review comments — and to anyone representing the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory on the repository, or
by the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if you would rather not use
GitHub. Reports go to the maintainer only.

Every report gets a reply. What happens next depends on what happened: it may
be a private word, an edit, a removed comment, or a block. The maintainer will
not discuss a reporter's identity with the person reported.
